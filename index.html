How It Works

From application to closing

Many of our loans close in 7 to 10 days, and we frequently step in with as little as 24 hours’ notice.

The process

  • Apply, and get your loan terms quickly
  • Send a few basic documents
  • We evaluate value: appraisal, BPO or internal
  • Title work and insurance, handled with your title company
  • Close within 1 business day of the valuation and clear title

What to have ready

  • The sales contract
  • A copy of your driver’s license or other ID
  • Corporate documentation
  • Addresses of any cross-collateral properties
  • Scope of work, if there is a renovation
